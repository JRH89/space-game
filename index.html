SPACE SHOOTER

ABOUT

A space shooter game built with Three.js.

Tech Stack: Three.js, Vanilla JS, CSS.

CREDITS

Spaceship Model: Free3D

Textures: Solar System Scope

HOW TO PLAY

Controls

Move: Arrow keys or WASD

Shoot: Space

Pause: Enter

Scoring

Meteor: 100 pts    Alien: 200 pts    Comet: 300 pts    Moon: 500 pts

Power-Ups

Extra Life, Health, Shield, Rapid Fire, 2x Score

Levels

Level up at: 1,000 / 2,500 / 5,000 points
